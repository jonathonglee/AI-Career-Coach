# Job description comparison
Read the job posting for required skills, preferred skills, duties, and qualifications. Compare each against specific resume evidence. Label an item as not demonstrated when the resume does not mention it; absence of evidence is not proof the candidate lacks the skill. Prioritize gaps that are essential to the role. Suggest legitimate projects, coursework, or learning activities to address gaps.

---

# Career exploration for students
Identify transferable skills from coursework, student organizations, volunteering, and part-time jobs. Compare career paths by daily responsibilities, required skills, and learning opportunities. Encourage informational interviews and review of several real job postings before choosing a specialization.

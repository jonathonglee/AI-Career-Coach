# Resume guidance: evidence and clarity
Use concise bullet points beginning with clear action verbs. Show what was done, how it was done, and the outcome when supported by facts. Quantify accomplishments only when the applicant knows the numbers. Avoid inventing metrics. Prioritize relevant experience and projects for the target position. A student can highlight class projects, volunteer work, leadership, and internships.

---

# Resume guidance: structure and readability
Use recognizable headings such as Education, Experience, Projects, and Skills. Keep dates and formatting consistent. Avoid dense paragraphs, decorative graphics, and confusing layouts. Proofread spelling and grammar. Tailor the wording to the job while remaining truthful. An automated screening system may parse plain, text-based resumes more reliably than complex layouts, but no formatting guarantees a result.

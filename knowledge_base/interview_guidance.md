# Behavioral interview preparation
Use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Prepare specific, honest examples about teamwork, problem solving, conflict, and adaptability. Focus on the student's own contributions. Do not fabricate work experiences or outcomes.

---

# Technical interview preparation
Use the actual job description to identify topics worth reviewing. Practice explaining projects: the problem, tools, design choices, tradeoffs, and results. When unsure of an answer, explain your reasoning and what you would check rather than bluffing.
